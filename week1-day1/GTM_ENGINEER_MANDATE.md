# GTM Engineer Mandate

## Introduction

An AI GTM Engineer sits at the intersection of revenue operations and software engineering. Instead of manually stitching together marketing, sales, and CRM tools by hand, this role designs automated systems where AI agents handle enrichment, scoring, and routing — so every lead moves through the funnel consistently, instantly, and without waiting on a human to triage it first.

## Responsibilities

The role centers on building and maintaining the automation layer of the revenue stack. Core responsibilities include: designing AI workflows that connect marketing capture to sales handoff; automating CRM record creation and updates so data stays current without manual entry; building lead enrichment pipelines that pull firmographic and intent data the moment a lead arrives; implementing AI scoring models that rank leads by fit and intent; wiring together APIs across tools like Clay, HubSpot, and Slack; automating repetitive workflow steps that previously required a human to click through them; and equipping sales with enablement tools — call summaries, lead context, and real-time notifications — so reps spend time selling, not searching for information.

## How AI Reduces CAC

Instead of manually qualifying 1,000 leads, AI enriches, scores, and routes them automatically. Enrichment agents pull company and contact data without a rep spending ten minutes per lead researching it. Scoring agents apply one consistent model across every lead instead of relying on inconsistent human judgment. Routing agents send only qualified leads to sales, so reps stop spending hours on unqualified contacts that were never going to convert. The combined effect: sales only contacts qualified leads, marketing spend goes further per closed deal, and the cost to acquire each customer drops — not because spend decreases, but because waste does.

## Increasing Pipeline Velocity

Without AI, a lead moves through manual research, a manual CRM update, manual assignment, and only then a sales call — a process that can take hours or days per lead. With AI, the same lead moves through enrichment in about 30 seconds, an AI score, an automatic CRM push, a Slack notification, and reaches sales almost immediately. This compressed sequence shortens the sales cycle end to end and increases conversion rates, because leads are contacted while their interest is still fresh rather than after a multi-hour or multi-day delay.

## Conclusion

AI GTM Engineers improve revenue operations by automating repetitive work, increasing sales efficiency, and enabling faster customer acquisition — turning a slow, manual funnel into a system that qualifies and routes leads in seconds instead of hours.
