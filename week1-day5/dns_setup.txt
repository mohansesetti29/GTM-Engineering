DAY 5 — EMAIL AUTHENTICATION DNS TEMPLATE
==========================================

IMPORTANT:
These are example/template records for learning.
Do NOT publish these records on a real domain without
checking your actual email provider's documentation.

SPF
---
Type: TXT
Host: @

Example:
v=spf1 include:spf.protection.outlook.com ~all


DKIM
----
Type: TXT
Host:
<selector>._domainkey

Value:
<provider-generated-DKIM-public-key>


DMARC
-----
Type: TXT
Host:
_dmarc

Example:
v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; pct=100;


PURPOSE
-------

SPF:
Specifies which sending infrastructure is authorized
to send mail for the domain.

DKIM:
Provides cryptographic authentication for outgoing
messages.

DMARC:
Defines how receiving systems should handle messages
that fail domain authentication/alignment checks.
